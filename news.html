September 5, 2026

Crisis Mapping Mapathon for beginner mappers on Day 1

In addition to the four mapping parties on SotM Asia Day 1 tomorrow, we will hold a Crisis Mapping Mapathon mainly for beginner mappers from Japan at AManTo Cafe 2F and online from 13:00 to 17:00 JST. The online core time is 13:30–14:30 JST. Focusing on flood-affected areas of Nepal, the event supports the development of remote volunteers who can update maps during disasters. We warmly invite you to join us.
