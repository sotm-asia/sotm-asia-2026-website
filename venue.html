Road to the Venue

Access Guide

  • About 15 minutes on foot from JR Osaka Station Midosuji Gate, Hankyu Osaka-Umeda Station, or Osaka Metro Umeda Station.
  • About 3 minutes on foot from Nakazakichō Station on the Osaka Metro Tanimachi Line.

Accessibility Information

  • Both Nakazakichō Hall and AManTo Cafe are wheelchair accessible.
  • Nakazakichō Station on the Osaka Metro Tanimachi Line is wheelchair accessible.
  • Osaka Station and Umeda Station are partially wheelchair accessible, but please note that accessible routes may require a considerable detour.

Nearby Accommodation

Nakazakichō Station on the Osaka Metro Tanimachi Line is the closest station, so staying along that line is especially convenient. Other central Osaka areas are also easy to access from, including locations within the JR Osaka Loop Line area and around Shin-Osaka Station.
